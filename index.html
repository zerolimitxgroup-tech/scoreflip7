How to Play

Goal

Be the first player to reach 200 points across multiple rounds. Each round, you draw cards trying to build a high score — but draw a duplicate number and you bust, scoring nothing.

On Your Turn

Hit — draw another card. Stay — stop and lock in your points for the round.

The Deck

This isn't a normal deck. There's only one 1, two 2s, three 3s, all the way up to twelve 12s. Higher cards are more common — but also more likely to bust you.

Busting

If you draw a number card that matches one already in front of you — you bust and score zero for the round.

Flip 7 Bonus

If you collect 7 different number cards, you score a +15 point bonus and the round ends immediately for everyone.

Scoring

Normal Mode

Add up your number cards. Apply ×2 to the number sum if you have it. Then add any + modifiers. Finally add +15 if you flipped 7.

Vengeance Mode Vengeance

Add up your number cards (0–13). Apply ×2 or ×3 if you have it. Then apply ÷2 (round down). Then subtract any negative modifiers (score can't go below 0). Then add positive modifiers. Finally add +15 if you flipped 7.

Vengeance Mode Cards New

Number Card: 13

13 cards with value 13 are added to the deck — high reward but high bust risk.

Lucky 13 🍀

You can hold two 13s without busting. Both score 13 and count toward your Flip 7. A third 13 would bust you.

Negative Modifiers: −2, −4, −6, −8, −10

These reduce your score. Applied after ÷2 but before positive modifiers.

÷2 Modifier

Cuts your number card total in half (rounded down). Applied before negative modifiers.

×3 Modifier

Triples your number card sum. Works like ×2 but bigger.

Winning

When at least one player reaches 200 points (or your custom target), the round finishes and the player with the highest score wins. Ties share the victory.
